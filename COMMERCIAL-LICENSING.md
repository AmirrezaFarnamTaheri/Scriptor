# Commercial licensing

Scriptor is available under **GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later**. The AGPL permits commercial use, modification, distribution, and network operation when its obligations are satisfied. The project does not add a non-commercial restriction to the AGPL grant.

A separate commercial license may be useful for an organization that wants to:

- distribute a proprietary product or closed-source derivative without AGPL obligations;
- operate a modified network service without providing corresponding source as required by the AGPL;
- obtain negotiated warranty, indemnity, support, service-level, integration, or redistribution terms.

A commercial license is an **alternative** to the AGPL, not a requirement for commercial activity that complies with the AGPL.
