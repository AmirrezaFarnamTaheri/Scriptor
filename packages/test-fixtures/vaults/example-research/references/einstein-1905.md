# Einstein 1905 — Photoelectric Effect

## Citation

Einstein, A. (1905). Über einen die Erzeugung und Verwandlung des Lichtes betreffenden heuristischen Gesichtspunkt. *Annalen der Physik*, 322(6), 132–148.

## Summary

Einstein proposed that light consists of discrete quanta (later called photons) and used this hypothesis to explain the photoelectric effect. This was one of the foundational papers of quantum mechanics.

### Key Points

- Light is emitted and absorbed in discrete packets (quanta)
- Energy of each quantum: $E = h\nu$ (Planck's constant × frequency)
- Explains why electron emission depends on frequency, not intensity
- Won the Nobel Prize in Physics (1921)
